Find it, name it, keep it

Every word you find puts the same question in front of you, which is how the genders end up sticking.

  1. Find the word

    Drag or tap across the grid. Words run horizontally, vertically and diagonally, and in both directions.

  2. Name its article

    Der, die or das — and for some words, the plural instead. The word is read aloud in German while you decide.

  3. Keep the points

    Each letter scores its German Scrabble value. Correct articles keep the points; wrong ones keep the word on the board.
