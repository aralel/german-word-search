Playing

How do I select a word?

Drag across the letters, or tap them one at a time. Words run horizontally, vertically and diagonally, and can be read in either direction. Tapping a selected tile again deselects it.

I found the word but got no points.

Points come from the article, not the word. After you find a word the app asks whether it's der, die or das — only a correct answer scores. A wrong one leaves the word on the board so you can try it again.

What do A1 to C2 mean?

They're the CEFR levels, from beginner (A1) to near-native (C2). Choosing a level includes every level below it, so a B1 puzzle can still contain A1 and A2 words. A1 and A2 are free; B1 and above are part of Premium.

Some words show a plural question instead of an article.

That's deliberate — a set of words is curated with verified plurals, and those occasionally ask for the plural form instead. It's the same scoring either way.

How do hints work?

A hint reveals the first letter of a word you haven't found. With Premium they're free and unlimited; without it, a hint follows a short video ad. Hints are tracked, so a puzzle finished with one doesn't count towards the "no hints" achievement.

What is the vocabulary notebook?

Every quiz answer adds that word to a Leitner spaced-repetition schedule: get it right and it comes back later, get it wrong and it comes back sooner. Open it from the menu to review whatever is due.

Premium and billing

I paid but Premium isn't active.

Open the paywall and choose Restore purchases, making sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. If it still doesn't unlock, email us — include the store receipt and we'll sort it out.

How do I cancel?

On iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → German Word Search → Cancel. On Android: Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are issued by Apple or Google, not by us — use reportaproblem.apple.com or the Play Store's order history. Tell us as well if something in the app caused it; we'd rather fix the cause.

Settings and data

How do I change the translation language?

Settings → Translation → Target language. Fifteen languages are available, and the status line underneath tells you whether the language is installed on your device or will be downloaded the first time it's used.

Can I turn off sound, vibration or data collection?

Yes — all three are switches in Settings. Data collection covers diagnostics and usage analytics; turning it off stops them at the source.

Does it work offline?

The game does: puzzles, quizzes, scoring and the notebook are all on-device. Ads, leaderboards, purchases and first-time translation-language downloads need a connection.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Everything — progress, statistics, notebook, settings — is stored on your device only, so removing the app removes all of it. Leaderboard entries live in your Game Center or Play Games account and are managed there.

I think an article is wrong.

Please report it. When a quiz answer is marked wrong, tap report wrong answer and confirm — the word, its meaning and the article the app gave come to us and we fix the list. These reports are the main way the word data improves.

Still stuck?

Write to support@aralel.com. Bug reports with a screenshot and the steps that led to it get fixed fastest.
